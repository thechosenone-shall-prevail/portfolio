Philosophy

The Hundred Cuts

Code isn't my identity. It's a tool, the same as Ghidra, WinDbg, or a hand-crafted LDAP filter. What I actually do is study how well-built enterprise systems fail, then build the one artifact that proves it in language the vendor and the responders are forced to act on.

I don't try to out-muscle a defense that was engineered by capable people. I read it until I find the one place its model of the world disagrees with the machine's. A volume string compared the wrong way. A file trusted a microsecond too long. A delegation edge nobody has looked at since 2004. I press on exactly that seam until the whole thing opens.

  • Kernel trust errors. Minifilters that make blocking decisions on the wrong volume path or an unsanitized user-mode pointer.
  • AD trust decay. Delegation and domain trusts designed two decades ago, defenseless against offline graph math.
  • Ring-0 severance. Endpoint agents that trust a signed legacy driver, so a single kernel write can sweep the whole EDR stack.
  • Adversarial extraction. Aligned models whose safety collapses under a budgeted campaign of distillation queries.
